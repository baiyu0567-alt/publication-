Getting started

The text comes out wrong, or nothing is recognised at all

Check the recognition language in Settings first. It is set to follow your system language by default, but it should match the language of the text you are photographing, not the language of the app. When the two disagree, recognition fails quietly rather than showing an error. If you read a lot of English text on a German phone, set the recognition language to English explicitly.

Recognition is poor on a particular page

Hold the phone parallel to the page rather than at an angle, fill the frame with the text, and avoid shadows falling across it. Glossy paper and low light are the two most common causes. If the magnifier is on, tapping the shutter captures the current view at the zoom level you have set.

Nothing happens when I share a link to the app

PresbyFriend extracts the main article text from a shared web address. Pages that load their content with JavaScript, or that sit behind a paywall or a login, often yield nothing to extract. You can also open the link in your browser, copy the text, and paste it into the app instead.

Subscriptions

What does Pro include?

The free version allows 10 readings per day. Pro removes that limit. There is one subscription with two billing periods — monthly and yearly — and both unlock the same thing.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by the store you bought from, not by us. On iOS, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions and select PresbyFriend. On Android, open the Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with.

I was charged but the app still says I'm on the free plan

Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out. Refunds are handled by the store, not by us — for iOS, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

PresbyFriend collects no data. Text recognition runs on your device, and nothing you photograph or open is uploaded. The full policy is here: Privacy Policy.

Accessibility

PresbyFriend is built for readers with presbyopia: large default type, high contrast, and a reading view with adjustable font size, line height, letter spacing, reading ruler, and four page themes (white, sepia, dark, yellow). If a setting doesn't do what you expect, that's worth an email — it's the part of the app we most want to get right.
